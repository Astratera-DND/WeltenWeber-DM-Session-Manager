# Projector and freeze

## 🚀 PROJECTOR (Ctrl+P)

Opens the **player view** full screen. If you have a second screen or a projector, it appears there. With only one
screen, it covers that one (press **Alt+Tab** to get back to your controls).

Everything you do on the stage, like fading layouts, moving pictures or drawing, shows live on the projector.

## Screen size: fit the stage to your projector

Your layouts are built on a **1920×1080 stage** (the green box in the preview). They never change. Where each picture sits
and how big it is are saved relative to that stage.

By default the tool **fits the stage to your projector screen**. Whether the projector is 1280×720, 1080p, 4K or 16:10, the whole
stage is scaled to fill it, with every picture in the same place, and the players never see anything outside the green box.
On a screen that isn't 16:9, you get black bars at the sides or at the top and bottom.

To show the stage pixel for pixel instead (like earlier versions), open **⚙ Settings** at the top and untick
**Fit the stage to the projector screen**. The setting is remembered.

![Settings](../images/settings.png)

Good to know: videos are always played at 720p quality, whatever you choose here. That keeps the tool smooth.
Scaling up to a 4K screen needs more processor power, so if playback gets choppy, untick the setting again.

## ❄️ FREEZE PROJECTOR

Want to prepare the next scene without your players watching you drag things around?

1. Press **FREEZE PROJECTOR**. The projector now shows a still picture of what it showed a moment ago.
2. Rearrange the stage in your preview as much as you like.
3. Press **🔄 SYNC PROJECTOR** (the same button) when you're ready. The projector jumps to the live stage.

## 🔄 RELOAD (Ctrl+R)

Rescans your folders. Use it after you add files with Windows Explorer or change library folders.
