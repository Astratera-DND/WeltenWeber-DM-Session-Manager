# Break mode and mini controls

## ☕ BREAK

Pizza time! One button gives your players something nice to look at, and gives you a pause.

**Press ☕ BREAK** and the tool:

1. remembers your current layout and track,
2. stops all music and SFX and clears the stage,
3. shows a **slideshow** of your break pictures (a new random one every 10 seconds),
4. plays a random track from your **break music** folder,
5. **pauses the recording** and writes break marks, if you are recording.

**Press ▶ RESUME** to come back: the slideshow and break music stop, your layout is back on the stage, the recording continues, and
your track starts again (from its beginning).

### Set up your break folders

Click the **⚙** next to the BREAK button and choose:

* **Break Music Folder:** a folder with some calm tracks.
* **Break Image Folder:** a folder with pictures (jpg, png, bmp, gif, webp).

If you leave a folder empty, that part is simply skipped.

## 🗔 Mini controls

A small window that always stays on top, perfect when you need to keep your notes in front but still change things.
Open it with **🗔 MINI CONTROLS** at the top right, and close it with the same button.

![Mini controls](../images/mini-controls.png)

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **⏭ NEXT LAYOUT** | Loads the next layout in your layouts list order. Under it you see which layout is next. |
| Layout dropdown | Jump to any layout |
| Scene dropdown | Jump to any scene (it also starts the scene with START ALL) |
| **🎬 NEXT SCENE** | Goes to the next scene in the list and starts it |
| **⏮ / ⏭** | Previous or next music track |
| **⏸ MUS / ⏸ SFX** | Pause or resume the music or the SFX |
| **📍 Mark** | Bookmark for the recording (active while you are recording) |
| Bottom lines | What's playing now and next, plus the time |
