# The main window at a glance

![Numbered main window](../images/main-window-numbered.png)

The window has four areas. Think of it as a stage with a control desk around it.

## Top: folder and record bar (1 and 2)

* **Sessions Folder:** the folder that holds your campaign. **📁 Browse…** lets you pick it.
* **🧳:** turns an older project into a portable one (more in [Moving to another PC](12-moving-between-pcs.md)).
* **🗔 MINI CONTROLS:** opens a small always-on-top window with the most-used buttons.
* **☕ BREAK** and **⏺ Record Session** with its buttons live in the bar below.

## Left: your campaign and your files (3 to 7)

* **Sessions and Scenes (3):** the folders of your campaign. Click to open.
* **Music (4), SFX (5), Visuals (6), Layouts (7):** the files of the current scene.
  Every panel has a **🔗** button (add files from somewhere else) and a **▼** button that folds the panel away.
  Fold the ones you don't need and the others get more room. The tool remembers which panels were folded.

## Middle: the stage (8 and 14)

The big black area is the **DM Preview**. It shows what the projector shows. The green dashed box marks the **projection
area** (what your players see). Above it:

![The foldable menus](../images/toolbar-menus.png)

* **🖌 Annotate (14):** a foldable menu with the **Brush**, **Rect** and **Clear** tools for drawing on the stage.
* **🔳 Blanking (14):** a foldable menu for black cover boxes. Blanking is **off by default**.
  See [Visuals, layouts and the stage](06-visuals-layouts-stage.md).
* Click a menu button to open or close it. A small **●** on the button tells you the tool is in use (drawing, or blanking is on),
  even while the menu is closed. The tool remembers which menus you left open.
* **Fade (s):** how long a layout change takes to fade.
* **Home:** snaps the view back so you see the whole green box.

## Right: the control desk (9 to 13)

* **Management (9):** save the layout, move pictures to the front or back, and the **👻 Ghost** slider: the see-through slider for the selected picture, video or blanking box.
* **Audio mixer (10):** now playing, play and stop, previous and next, In Order or Shuffle, volume sliders.
* **MASTER PLAY / STOP:** start or stop all audio.
* **Projector buttons (11):** open the projector, freeze it, reload the folders.
* **Scripts (12)** and **START ALL (13)**.
